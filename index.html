Warehouse Operations & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Comprehensive, step-by-step operating guidelines for receiving shipments, picking orders, internal rack transfers, cycle auditing, SKU master governance, and security controls.

SOP 01
Inbound Shipment Receiving & Stock Intake (+ Intake)
Available to: All Operators

Execute this standard operating procedure whenever new vendor shipments, supplier purchase orders, or cross-docked goods arrive at the inbound warehouse dock.

1 Launch Intake Modal

Click the green + Add Stock button in the top navigation bar, or press hotkey I.

2 Select SKU & Destination Bay

Select the catalog item from the dropdown and type the destination Bay / Bin (e.g. A-01-01 or RECEIVING-DOCK).

3 Input Units & Purchase Order #

Enter the verified physical unit count. Enter the supplier invoice, PO #, or carrier Bill of Lading (BOL) in the Reference field.

4 Commit & Auto-Audit

Click Receive & Stock. On-hand inventory increments immediately and creates an immutable row in the Inventory History audit log.

💡
Best Practice: Double-check the barcode label on the physical pallet before confirming putaway to prevent misplacing mixed SKU batches in high-density rack zones.
SOP 02
Outbound Order Picking & Inventory Dispatch (− Dispatch)
Available to: All Operators

Follow this procedure when picking stock for outbound customer sales orders, manufacturing work orders, or inter-facility transfers.

1 Locate Stock in Table

On the Inventory On Hand view, use the live search bar or category filters to find the specific SKU and location.

2 Click − Subtract Action

Click the red − Subtract button on that row, or use the − quantity stepper for single-unit picks.

3 Specify Units & Order Reference

Enter the number of units being dispatched. Type the customer order number, packing slip #, or carrier tracking ID.

4 Confirm & Low-Stock Trigger

Click Dispatch Units. If the remaining count drops to or below the minimum Reorder Point, a yellow Low Stock badge appears automatically.

⚠️
Safety Warning: You cannot dispatch more units than are currently on hand in that location. If stock is insufficient, check other warehouse bays or perform a location transfer.
SOP 03
Internal Location Relocation & Bay Transfers (⇄ Move)
Available to: All Operators

Use internal transfers when reorganizing warehouse aisles, moving pallets from receiving staging to permanent bulk storage, or consolidating partial cartons.

1 Select Source Bay

Find the item in the inventory list and click the blue ⇄ Move button under Quick Actions.

2 Enter Destination Bay

Type the target location code (e.g., from STAGE-01 to B-04-02).

3 Specify Units to Relocate

Enter the exact quantity being moved. You can transfer full pallets or split partial cartons.

4 Atomic Database Move

Click Complete Transfer. The system automatically reduces from the source location and adds to the destination location in a single atomic transaction.

💡
Consolidation Rule: If the destination bay already holds that same SKU, Simpletory will automatically merge the quantities together instead of creating duplicate records.
SOP 04
Physical Cycle Counting & Inventory Reconciliation (⚙ Adjust)
Available to: All Operators

Perform periodic physical cycle counts to verify that on-hand inventory levels match physical rack contents, preventing stockouts and shrinkage.

1 Initiate Bay Count

Locate the bay row undergoing audit and click the ⚙ Adjust button under Quick Actions.

2 Perform Physical Count

Physically count all units on the shelf or pallet. Check for damaged or un-scanned items.

3 Enter Count & Variance Reason

Input the verified physical quantity into the form and provide a clear explanation (e.g. "Q4 Cycle Count", "Found 2 damaged boxes").

4 Save Reconciled Count

Click Save Reconciled Count. The system records the previous quantity, the new quantity, and the exact delta in the permanent audit trail.

📊
Accountability: The Inventory History log records the user's name, timestamp, and variance delta so management can track shrinkage trends across specific warehouse zones.
SOP 05
Master SKU Catalog & Product Definition Management
Restricted to: Superadmin & Manager

Catalog management defines the master item records, classification hierarchy, units of measure, and safety stock reorder thresholds for your facility.

1 Open Item Catalog View

Click Item Catalog in the left sidebar (or press C), then click the + New SKU button.

2 Define SKU & Description

Enter the unique SKU identifier (e.g. SKU-5001) and an item description. SKUs must be unique within each tenant facility.

3 Assign Categories & UOM

Select or type the Category, Sub-Category, and Unit of Measure (UOM) (EA, CS, PL, RL, PK, etc.).

4 Set Reorder Point & Unit Cost

Enter the minimum safety stock threshold (reorder point) and the replacement unit cost for inventory valuation reports.

💡
Catalog vs Inventory: The Item Catalog defines what a product is, while Inventory On Hand tracks where and how many units exist in physical warehouse racks.
SOP 06
Multi-Facility Management & Active/Inactive Isolation Rules
Restricted to: Superadmin Only

Simpletory supports complete multi-facility isolation. Superadmins can manage multiple distribution centers, toggle facilities active/inactive, and control site visibility.

1 Access Facility Control

Navigate to System Settings (press S) and scroll to the Warehouse Facilities & Tenant Isolation Control panel.

2 Add New Facilities

Click + Add Facility, assign a name (e.g. "West Coast Distribution Center"), and define an isolation code (e.g. org-west).

3 Toggle Active / Inactive Status

Use the Deactivate / Activate action button on any facility row to immediately toggle its operational status in real-time.

4 Automatic Auto-Hiding

Inactive facilities are instantly hidden from all login selectors, top header facility switchers, and operator dropdowns across all devices.

🔒
Security Protection: If a standard operator belonging to an inactive facility tries to sign in, access is blocked with a security notice directing them to contact their Superadmin.
SOP 07
User Roles, Access Permissions & Security Governance
Overview

Simpletory enforces a strict 3-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model, automated 30-minute idle session timeouts, and cryptographic SHA-256 password protection.

Superadmin
  • Global multi-warehouse switching
  • Supabase cloud database configuration
  • Create / Deactivate warehouse facilities
  • Global user & team management
  • Full inventory and catalog control
Manager
  • Manage all operations within assigned facility
  • Create & edit master catalog SKUs
  • Manage facility team members & operators
  • Perform stock intake, pick, transfers, audits
  • Restricted from global settings & other sites
Standard User / Operator
  • View on-hand inventory in assigned facility
  • Perform receiving (+ Add) and picking (− Pick)
  • Execute rack transfers (⇄ Move) and cycle audits
  • View change history audit trail
  • Restricted from SKU master & settings
⏱️
Inactivity Timeout: For compliance and floor security, if an operator is idle for 30 minutes without mouse/keyboard interaction, the session terminates automatically and requires fresh login.
SOP 08
Inventory Change History, Audit Logs & Traceability
Available to: All Operators

Every single stock modification (Add, Subtract, Move, Adjust, Transfer) creates a permanent, immutable record in the database audit log.

1 Open Change History

Click Change History in the sidebar (or press H) to view the real-time audit stream.

2 Filter by Action Type

Use the action filter dropdown to isolate specific transaction types (ADD, SUBTRACT, ADJUST, TRANSFER).

3 Inspect Delta Quantities

Review the exact Previous Quantity, Delta (+/−), and resulting New Quantity for every transaction.

4 Operator Accountability

Each entry records the operator's full name, timestamp, location, and reason notes for complete compliance.
